## 1. Executive summary

**What LinkPlease says it is:** *"Instagram DM automation with an AI safety layer. Built on Meta's official Graph API as a Verified Tech Provider."* Its positioning is deliberately narrower than Zorcha's: *"LinkPlease is built for one job — turning Instagram engagement into DMs that convert"* — marketed as *"creator-first automation without the flow-builder tax."* ([Home](https://linkplease.co/), [vs ManyChat](https://linkplease.co/compare/manychat))

**Scale it claims:** 45K+ users, 12+ countries, 150M+ DMs sent, 50M+ comments handled, 15M+ followers gained. Its LinkedIn page says "Trusted by 1000+ creators" — a large discrepancy with the homepage; treat both as unverified marketing numbers. ([Home](https://linkplease.co/), [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/company/linkplease))

**Core wedge:** *"Free forever. No credit card."* with 1,000 DMs/month and 1,000 contacts, then a **flat per-account price** (₹399/mo Pro) with unlimited DMs, automations and contacts — explicitly contrasted with ManyChat's per-contact billing and LinkDM's monthly DM caps. ([Pricing](https://linkplease.co/pricing), [vs ManyChat](https://linkplease.co/compare/manychat))

**Business model:** Free → Pro ₹399 → Super ₹799 (AI layer) → Max ₹899 (AI + protection) → Enterprise custom, plus an affiliate program (25% recurring for 11 months) and an influencer program (free Max + 25% for 6 months + cash incentives). Prices are **per Instagram account**, INR-first with USD toggle and En/Pt/Es language switcher.

**Strategic difference from Zorcha:** LinkPlease competes on **simplicity + an "AI safety" identity** rather than breadth. It has no visual Flow Builder, no AI FAQ knowledge base, no Link-in-Bio, no ads automation, no MCP, and no webinars/events/bundles. Its own differentiators are **Live automation, Share-to-DM, AI comment moderation, a brand-deal (Collab) detector, and a prominent Safety Engine**.

## 7. How LinkPlease actively acquires customers (reverse-engineered playbook)

1. **Free-forever with a hard-but-honest cap.** "1,000 DMs / 1,000 contacts, no credit card" lets users start instantly, while the cap creates a natural upgrade trigger ("upgrade when your comments outgrow you"). Contrast with Zorcha's *unlimited* wedge.
2. **Flat-price attack on two enemies.** Two comparison pages — vs ManyChat (per-contact billing, "flow-builder tax") and vs LinkDM (monthly DM caps) — with full feature tables, dated "last checked July 2026" notes, an explicit "Not documented ≠ doesn't exist" fairness disclaimer, switching FAQs, and a "recreate triggers in minutes" migration story.
3. **AI-safety identity as a category.** "AI safety layer," a live Safety Engine dashboard, IG Shield, AI rotation, Comment Guard — this gives a memorable reason to switch that isn't just price.
4. **Two-tier creator incentive machine.** Affiliate: 25% recurring commission for 11 months, earnings calculator, instant withdrawal. Influencer: minimum 3 months of Max free, 25% for 6 months, cash incentives, 2-minute application form asking for IG handle + follower count + WhatsApp number.
5. **Proof with numbers, not badges alone.** Named creators with follower counts and testimonials claiming "6000+ followers in 20 days" and "10k+ followers in one month."
6. **Support as marketing.** "A real person replies," "customer support is the real USP" — used on homepage, pricing and comparison pages.
7. **Localization.** INR-first pricing with USD toggle, En/Pt/Es language switcher, GST invoices, WhatsApp-number lead capture — India-first with global ambition (12+ countries claimed).
8. **Simplicity CTA.** "Set up your first automation in two minutes" repeated on every page.

## 9. What to do to match LinkPlease and acquire its customers

### A. Product moves (small, high leverage)

1. **Ship "Comment Guard" (AI moderation)** — auto-hide spam, hate, competitor promos, with a review log. It's a headline Super-plan feature for LinkPlease and cheap to build once T2.3 AI exists.
2. **Ship a Collab/Brand-deal detector** — notify the creator the moment a brand DMs them about a partnership. Strong emotional hook ("never miss a brand deal"), low technical complexity, and Zorcha only has a passive "Collabs" profile.
3. **Productize your safety engine as "Shield"** — you already have caps, queue, jitter, variants, spike control and `safety_events`. Wrap it in a visible, honest live dashboard (queue depth, sends/min vs Meta limit, breaches = 0) and market it like LinkPlease's IG Shield. This is your strongest shared narrative with LinkPlease — but yours is measured, not a marketing mockup.
4. **Keep the 2-minute setup path.** LinkPlease's core positioning is "no node-and-arrow builder"; your T2.2 rule already says keep Quick Automations default. Market that explicitly: *"Live on your next post, not next week — advanced flows optional."*

6. **Add digital products / sell-in-DM (T3.5)** if monetization is your strategy — but note LinkPlease's homepage showcase is not mirrored on its pricing page, so verify before using it as a Zorcha-style commerce claim.
7. **WhatsApp automation (T3.2)** — neither LinkPlease (support channel only) nor Zorcha has it. Your strongest moat against both.

## 11. Caveats — read before repeating any claim

- **All numbers are self-reported** (45K users, 150M DMs, 15M followers gained, 6000+ followers in 20 days). The homepage's 45K+ conflicts with LinkedIn's "1000+ creators" — treat both as marketing.
- **Digital Products is inconsistent**: prominent on the homepage, absent from all pricing plan lists. The pricing page is the more reliable source for what's included.
- **Comparison data is dated**: both comparison pages say competitor details were "last checked July 2026," and independent reviewers note LinkPlease's ManyChat row predates ManyChat's March 2026 active-contact pricing change. Don't reuse those competitor figures as current facts.
- **"Not documented" is LinkPlease's own fair-use wording** on its comparison pages — mirror that honesty rather than claiming competitors lack features.
- **"Priority WhatsApp support" is a support channel**, not WhatsApp automation. Do not misread it as omnichannel messaging.
- **`linkplease.co.in` is a different product shape** (separate branding, "Global Instagram DM automation," Hindi/Hinglish AI replies, UPI sell-in-DM, Gmail Assistant, iOS/Android coming soon, different pricing and URLs). Its relationship to `linkplease.co` is **unconfirmed** — do not merge their claims. Analyze them as two distinct properties if you target the `.co.in` audience.
- **Brand/legal:** "match LinkPlease" means reaching its product standard and winning its users — not impersonating its name, logos, testimonials or Meta-partner status. Build dmplease as your own brand.

## 12. One-line takeaway

LinkPlease wins on a free-with-a-cap wedge, flat per-account pricing, a memorable "AI safety layer" identity, and two sharp comparison pages plus a creator incentive machine — but it has no flow builder, no AI FAQ, no content/SEO library, and a hard 1,000-DM free ceiling, which are exactly the openings dmplease should attack with honest safety analytics, real conversion metrics, optional depth, and WhatsApp as a moat.
