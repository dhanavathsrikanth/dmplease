## 1. Executive summary

**What Zorcha says it is:** an "AI-powered automation platform built to help creators, brands and agencies transform Instagram engagement into direct conversations," with a bigger ambition to become *"the operating system for anyone trying to build in the attention economy"* and the tagline **"Where you win the Attention Game."** ([About](https://zorcha.com/about), [Homepage](https://zorcha.com/))

**Scale it claims:** 100,000+ creators across 60+ countries; 100 million messages sent every month; 1,127,009 "millions of words generated using AI FAQ." Launched 25 Dec 2024; 5K active users Jan 2025 → 15K Mar 2025 → 100K+ today. ([About](https://zorcha.com/about), [Homepage](https://zorcha.com/), [AI FAQ](https://zorcha.com/products/ai-faq))

**Core wedge:** *"Unlimited Free DM Automations"* — unlimited automations, unlimited DMs, unlimited contacts, free. Against competitors priced at "$15/month," Zorcha positions itself as democratizing access. ([Free Unlimited](https://zorcha.com/products/free-unlimited/))

**Business model:** free tier + 3 paid tiers ($14.99 / $39.99 / $109.99 per month) + per-transaction platform fee on digital products (8% / 5% / 3% / 1%) + Cashfree's separate 2% payment fee + separate AI credits + affiliate program. ([Pricing](https://zorcha.com/pricing))

**How it acquires customers:** free-unlimited wedge → comparison SEO → use-case SEO → content/SEO blog → creator affiliate army → trust badges + case studies → events/community → product-led sharing (public reports, templates, QR tests) → AI-assistant distribution via MCP.
